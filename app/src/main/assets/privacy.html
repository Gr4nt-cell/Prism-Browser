Privacy Policy

iBrowser (package com.gr4nt.browser) · Effective 20 October 2026

The short version

  • iBrowser has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We (the developer) do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your personal data.
  • Your history, bookmarks, tabs, downloads and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • iBrowser only talks to the internet when you visit websites or use a feature that needs it (listed below).

1. Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, iBrowser keeps the following in its private app storage on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you share or back it up yourself:

  • Browsing history, open tabs (and small screenshots of them for the tab switcher), the Archive of closed tabs, bookmarks, Favorites and Reading List.
  • Downloaded files and the download list (file names, links and progress).
  • Website data saved by the sites you visit: cookies, local storage and cache — handled by Android's built-in WebView, like any browser.
  • Your settings, including per-site camera / microphone / location choices, ad-blocking choices and the ad-blocking filter lists.
  • Where you stopped playing long videos, so playback can resume.

Private tabs don't save history, aren't archived and don't remember site permissions; on Android System WebView versions that support separate profiles, their cookies and site data are kept apart from normal tabs and wiped when you close your last private tab.

You can delete this information at any time in Settings › Clear History and Website Data, in the History and Archive screens, or by uninstalling the app.

2. When iBrowser connects to the internet

Websites you visitPages, images, videos and files are loaded from the websites you open, which receive your IP address and the usual browser information, and may set their own cookies. Each website's own privacy policy applies.
Search suggestionsWhile you type in the address bar (outside private tabs), what you type is sent to the search engine you chose in Settings (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, Ecosia or Yahoo) to get suggestions, and your searches go to that engine.
Ad-blocking listsAbout every four days iBrowser downloads public filter lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock, AdGuard, Peter Lowe's list, URLhaus and others you enable). No information about you is sent — only a normal download request.
Downloads & mediaFiles and video streams are downloaded directly from the server that hosts them, using the website's cookies when needed so the download works.
Translate & image searchOnly when you choose "Translate Page", the page address is opened in Google Translate. Only when you choose "Search Image with Google Lens", the image address is sent to Google Lens.

Pinch to Summarize, Reader view, ad blocking and media detection all run on your phone — page content is not sent anywhere for these features.

3. Permissions and why they're used

InternetTo load websites and downloads.
Camera & microphoneOnly when a website asks (video calls, voice search, QR codes, photo uploads) and only after you tap Allow. iBrowser itself never records anything.
LocationOnly when a website asks (for example maps) and you allow it. iBrowser doesn't store or send your location.
Files & storage, all-files accessTo save downloads to the folder you choose and to open files you pick. Optional.
NotificationsTo show download progress and media playback controls.
Foreground service, wake lockSo downloads and audio keep going when the app is in the background.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings › Apps › iBrowser › Permissions, and reset site choices in iBrowser Settings › Websites.

4. Data sharing, selling and security

We don't operate servers that receive your data, so there is nothing for us to share or sell. iBrowser contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Connections to websites use HTTPS whenever the website supports it, and iBrowser refuses to load pages with invalid security certificates. Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox.

5. Children

iBrowser is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Because your information is stored only on your device, you are in full control: you can view it in the app, delete it in Settings, or remove everything by uninstalling iBrowser. We hold no copy of your data that could be requested, corrected or deleted.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated and the effective date above changed before the new version is released.
